Mechanical Engineering · SUSTech

I’m an undergraduate at Southern University of Science and Technology. I work on dexterous manipulation, teleoperation, and robot learning.

My recent projects include CRAFT-Wrist, a two-axis wrist for a tendon-driven hand, and a browser interface for Dummy V2 that connects a robot model to joint control and motion planning.
